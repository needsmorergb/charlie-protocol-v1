What a destination means
The first row is filled in with 1nc1nerator11111111111111111111111111111111, Solana's incinerator. Its source says lamports credited there are removed from the total supply at the end of the block. SOL sent there is destroyed, not stored, and that is what deflation means.
Any other address you put here simply receives the SOL. Only the incinerator reduces the supply, so if that is what you want, keep it in the split.
A destination that buys the token and destroys it reduces the coin's own supply instead. Anything else is an ordinary wallet, and the coin's page says so in those words.
