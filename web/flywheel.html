The flywheel, executed

A fee arrives. The program splits it four ways: a quarter to buying and burning $CHARLIE, and the rest to the three legs the coin's own dev chose. No key the dev or Charlie holds can redirect any of it. Below is that happening on a chain, with signatures.

This is devnet, and one step of it is mocked. The program below is deployed and the transactions below landed: the split, the destinations and the refusals are real, and every signature is checkable. But pump does not exist on devnet, so the creator fee arriving at the collector is an ordinary transfer rather than pump paying an enrolled coin.

What this proves is the mechanism. It proves nothing about volume, price, or what any coin would earn, and no such figure appears on this page.

Anyone can turn it

distribute takes no signer. The round below was called by a wallet that is neither the coin's admin nor its ops address — it has no claim on any leg — and the toll moved anyway:

  • caller: 67oped...KBt8, admin: no, ops: no
  • the toll it moved: 100,000 lamports, and charlie_pool gained exactly that (100,000)
  • what the caller was paid: nothing. Its balance changed by -5,000 lamports — it paid the transaction fee and received no share of any leg
  • the transaction

That last line is deliberate, not an oversight. A fixed tip for calling the crank would be an extractable bounty: whoever called it would be paid out of the toll and both burn legs, so the legs would fund their own collection. The crank's incentive is the ops share and the protocol's own operation.

What a caller cannot do

The guarantee is an absence, and an absence is only worth stating if you try the thing and print the refusal. Each of these was submitted to the deployed program:

  • ✓ a caller substitutes their own ops address — refused
    distribute: ops account is not the one route names
    ops_address is read out of route(mint), never taken from the caller
  • ✓ a caller substitutes the incinerator — refused
    distribute: the SOL burn leg pays the incinerator, and nothing else
    the SOL burn leg pays the incinerator, a constant in the program
  • ✓ a dev sets their own shares to the whole fee — refused
    route: the three dev shares must sum to 10000 - TOLL_BPS
    the three dev shares must sum to 10000 - TOLL_BPS, enforced on every write
  • ✓ the dev moves their own three shares around — accepted
    route updated
    a split that leaves the toll alone is the dev's to choose, any day

What this is not

It is not mainnet, it is not enrollment, and it is not a claim that any coin earns anything. The creator fee arriving was a transfer, not pump. The program is upgradeable, which means the absences above are today's absences and not yet permanent — revoking upgrade authority is the one-way door that makes them guarantees, and it comes after the pipeline has run in production, not before.
