## Thread A — last night: the SOL burn leg started burning SOL

**A1**

morning. last night the SOL burn leg started actually burning SOL. it did not
before. the old standard was "unspendable", and unspendable is a freeze, not a
burn: lamports nobody can spend still exist, and total supply never moved.
calling that deflation was false.

**A2**

the destination is now Solana's incinerator.

1nc1nerator11111111111111111111111111111111

from the runtime's own source: "Lamports credited to this address will be
removed from the total supply (burned) at the end of the current block."

that is the chain's guarantee, not ours.

**A3**

the check had to invert with it. SOL_BURN_BALANCE asked sum(inflows) ==
balance. against the incinerator that reads X == 0 and would have branded every
correctly burning coin red.

now the balance MUST be zero. non-zero is the anomaly. unread is UNCHECKED,
never a pass.

**A4**

the zero is stronger evidence than the equality it replaced. a vault holding
the SOL proves only that the SOL is sitting there. a balance that returns to
zero after lamports were credited proves they left the supply.

**A5**

and the trap right next to it, written down so nobody learns it the expensive
way: sending an SPL TOKEN to the incinerator does not burn it. the runtime
destroys lamports, not token balances. token supply falls through the token
program's burn instruction. that is the other leg.

## Thread B — last night: enrollment is live on mainnet

**B1**

also live as of last night: a dev connects a wallet, proves ownership, sets
their fee split. on mainnet, today. no program of ours is deployed and none is
needed. pump's fee-share program already owns the split, and we call the same
instruction its own UI calls.

**B2**

every byte of that message is derived from pump's on-chain IDL, read from the
IDL account rather than from documentation. discriminator, account list AND its
order, PDA seeds, layout. two things the IDL does not record we found by
simulating against mainnet instead of guessing.

**B3**

the rule that matters most: pump lets a coin change its split exactly ONCE.
spend it by accident and it cannot be undone.

so the page says that before the form, the API refuses a coin that already used
it, and no transaction reaches a wallet that has not simulated clean first.

**B4**

nothing we run signs anything. the API returns an unsigned message and your
wallet signs it. the page ships no bundled Solana library, so there is no third
party code between you and what you are signing. it is the one page with
script, and it is written out rather than minified.

**B5**

when you send, you land on /verify/<your mint>. the page that reads your split
back off the chain and runs the checks against it. that is the page worth
sharing.

enrollment ends where verification starts, on purpose.

## Thread E — the job, and why Solana should care

**E1**

what the job actually is. pump prints coins all day and the creator fees mostly
land in wallets. we route a share of that flow into the incinerator, where the
runtime deletes it from the total supply.

memecoin volume becomes SOL that no longer exists.

**E2**

we do not promise you a price. we promise the supply goes down and that you can
check it.

fewer SOL against the same demand is not a narrative, it is arithmetic. the
half we are accountable for is the first one, and every number we publish
carries a check allowed to fail.

**E3**

why the checking half matters to Solana more than the burning half: every chain
has burn dashboards, most of them are wrong, and each wrong one costs the word
"deflationary" a little more.

if "burned" is a claim anyone can recompute, deflation becomes a fact here.

**E4**

one coin doing this is a gimmick. the standard is the point. every pump coin
that enrolls routes a share of its fees out of the supply for good, and does it
in a way a stranger can check without asking us anything.

that is the part Solana keeps after the cycle.

**E5**

$CHARLIE keeps the hardest version of it. our own coin routes to a legacy
shared address, admin revoked, unspendable but not burned. it fails our
standard on its own terms and we publish no SOL burn total for it.

that is on the live site, not in a footnote.

**E6**

launched a pump coin? /enroll, connect the admin wallet, set your split,
remember you get one change.

want to check somebody else's burn claim? paste any contract address into
/verify.
